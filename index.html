Derrick Quinn

Ph.D. Student, Computer Systems Laboratory (CSL), Cornell University

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Computer Systems Laboratory (CSL) at the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Cornell University, where I am advised by Professor Mohammad Alian. My research interests are centered around the design and programming of distributed and heterogeneous computer systems. Specifically, I focus on hardware-software co-design for data-intensive applications, with a particular emphasis on databases and AI/ML. I am also interested in exploring methods for cost-effective, time-efficient, and energy-efficient scaling of computer systems.
